IEEE SRDS 2025
Randomized vs. Deterministic? Practical Randomized Synchronous BFT in Expected Constant Time
Xufeng Zhang, Baohan Huang, Sisi Duan, Haibin Zhang
[DOI] [eprint(full paper)]

In this paper, we present a new randomized synchronous BFT paradigm with optimal resilience, expected O(1) time, and amortized O(n) message per block proposal, against strongly rushing adaptive adversaries. The paradigm reduces BFT to a new variant of multi-valued agreement having the non-intrusion property. To instantiate our BFT protocol, we also provide a binary agreement protocol and a multi-valued agreement protocol with fewer steps than those of Katz & Koo (CRYPTO 06) and ADDNR (FC 21).

ACISP 2024
Enhancing Permissioned Blockchains with Controlled Data Authorization
Qichang Liu, Xufeng Zhang, Sisi Duan, Haibin Zhang
[DOI] [PDF]

In this paper, we present a new primitive called threshold encryption with controlled authorization, combining threshold encryption and threshold proxy re-encryption. It allows a writer to encrypt messages on the decouples data encryption from later data authorization in permissioned blockchains to achieve distributed fine-grained access control.
